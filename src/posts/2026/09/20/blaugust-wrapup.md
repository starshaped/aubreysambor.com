I participated in [Blaugust](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/) for the first time this year and [wrote every day for 31 days](/tags/blaugust2026/)! I'm still surprised and proud of myself for doing it, and it's the most I've blogged in a long, long time. As you can see, though, I'm writing this post nearly three weeks after the end of August, so I have other things to say about the month long event.

While I'm proud of myself for blogging every day, blogging every day also burned me out. The first part of Blaugust went well for me, as I had so many ideas for things to post. However, in the middle of the month, I ran out of ideas and struggled a LOT with keeping up. I spent most evenings stressing out over what to post the next day which didn't feel good to me. I want blogging to be an enjoyable activity and not an obligation.

It didn't help that my contract job became really busy, so the last thing I wanted to do after spending a day staring at a computer for 8 hours was spend even MORE time in front of a screen in the evening. It's honestly why I struggle in general with blogging, and I've found that I can't sit at my desk and work on my site or blog because it feels too much like work. I'm writing this post on my couch and hopefully that will be the key to writing more consistently.

I'm not sure I'll do Blaugust next year. I want to figure out a better blogging cadence and I want to find a way to blog without feeling so mentally exhausted from being in front of a computer, and hopefully I can figure something out soon. I don't want to go months without blogging again, but I'm not sure if blogging every day works for me either. We'll see what I do.
