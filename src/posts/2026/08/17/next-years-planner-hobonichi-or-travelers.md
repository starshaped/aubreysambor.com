For the past 6 years, I've been using Hobonichi planners to keep my personal life organized. I started by using an [A6 Techo](https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/lineup#original) as my planner in 2021 and 2022, and switched to a [Hobonichi Weeks](https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/lineup#weeks) starting in 2023. I love my Weeks, but I'm considering making a change for 2027.

***

I don't remember when I first heard about [Traveler's Notebooks](https://www.jetpens.com/blog/TRAVELER-S-COMPANY-TRAVELER-S-notebook-A-Comprehensive-Guide/pt/726). I remember seeing them in stationery stores I've gone to but I remember not quite understanding the point and feeling overwhelmed by the sheer amount of paper options for this particular planner system.

This past year, however, I started hearing more about them and how people use them, either by reading blog posts or watching Youtube videos of people flipping through their setups. Suddenly, the appeal of a Traveler's Notebook made sense to me.

The Traveler's Notebook is a modular planner system that consists of a leather cover and elastic binding to add [add multiple refills to your planner](https://www.jetpens.com/blog/TRAVELER-S-COMPANY-TRAVELER-S-notebook-A-Comprehensive-Guide/pt/726#Midori%20Diary%20Paper) depending on how you want to use it. You can buy planner inserts similar to the Weeks layouts to use it as a traditional planner, you can buy blank journal paper, you can buy craft paper notebooks if you do mixed media journaling, and so on.

This idea of switching out notebooks depending on your use really appeals to me, as I'd love to keep a more comprehensive journal for when I travel. I want to travel with both a planner and a travel journal, so I'd be able to carry around both in the same leather cover if I decided to go with Travelers for 2027.

However, it appears that Hobonichi is releasing a similar system in 2027 called '[collect & collage](https://www.1101.com/store/techo/en/magazine/contents/collectandcollage/b6e71e6s2.html)' which for now looks to be more of a way to document travels and such in a little scrapbook. It's not looking to be the same as a Traveler's Notebook, however, so I'm still looking into Travelers first and foremost as a system I want to try next year.

Who am I kidding, though, I'll still buy a Hobonichi Weeks! I might buy the Travelers just to play with it in 2027 and if I want to make the full leap, I'll do so in 2028. Either way, I'm excited about planner season and can't wait to see what both Hobonichi and Traveler's Company release for next year.
