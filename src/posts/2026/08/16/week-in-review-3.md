Happy Sunday, everyone! I've had a pretty busy week this week with knit night, D&D, and the [Analog Tools meetup](https://cygnoir.net/analog-tools-meetups). I'm always happy to be connecting with people, whether it be in person (knit night), or online (D&D and Analog Tools). I'm grateful to share interests with so many cool people and I'm glad I've found these groups!

On to this week's recap!

## Making
I'm still working on the [Lillesol sweater](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/lillesol) and I'm still happy with [my progress](/wip-wednesday-lillesol-progress/). I'm at the boring part of this sweater where I just knit round and round until I get to the bottom, then I'll pick up stitches for the neckline and sleeves. And then it will be round and round and round again!

## Watching
I'm still watching Big Brother 28 and still listening to all the podcasts. It takes up a lot of time but I find it utter fascinating. It's a silly show that doesn't take itself seriously and I'm really digging it.

I'm also still watching season 20 of Married at First Sight and finished Survivor: Pearl Islands. Nothing much else to say about TV as I'm so busy with podcasts!

## Reading
I started reading [The Spellshop](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/84d9df4f-a582-4550-83d7-f9d9d5c0c47f) and it is definitely more cozy and romance-ey than most books I read. I'm fine with it because I've read a lot of books with heavier subject matter recently, so I'm along for the ride with this one!

No progress on [The Lion Women of Tehran](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/54d49b26-f846-4c43-a2dd-2031c3750f97). One day I'll finish it.

## Listening
I've mainly been listening to podcasts, but I'm also listening to the [BIRP! August 2026 Indie Playlist](https://birp.fm/playlist/2026/august-2026/). I've been listening to BIRP! playlists since 2009 or 2010 and I'm so glad they're still around in 2026! I'm particularly enjoying [Wishy](https://wishy.bandcamp.com/album/natures-pill) and [CASTLEBEAT](https://castlebeat.bandcamp.com/album/castlebeat-ii) and am looking forward to Wishy's new album in October.

I also listened to [Group Therapy 688 by Above & Beyond](https://music.apple.com/us/album/group-therapy-688-dj-mix/6796341151) on my drive to and from knit night earlier the week because I love driving to electronic music!

## Fitness
I ran a little more this week than I did last week, getting out there twice in the mornings to run a little over 2 miles each time. I'm slowly picking up the pace and it's humbling to see how much slower I am now than I was 12-13 years ago in my early 30s. What matters is that I'm getting out there and not how fast I run, so I try to remember that.

I also did some bodyweight strength training twice this week. My routine consists of backwards lunges, ab crunches, wall pushups (for now because I can't do a real pushup), planks, and squats. I'm working toward doing regular pushups, which is a big goal of mine because I've never been able to do a pushup. I can do it.

## Blogging
Again, [I've blogged every day this week](/tags/blaugust2026/)! Blogging this week was way harder than blogging the first week, but I know that's how month long challenges go as I ran into the same thing when I did NaNoWriMo (National Novel Writing Month) in the past. Consistency is key and my goal is to stick with it, and here I am, 16 days into Blaugust and I'm still going strong. I'm proud of myself.
