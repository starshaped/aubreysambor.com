It's August 4th and I haven't yet introduced myself to anyone new who could be reading my blog. Thanks, [Wry Writer](https://wrywriter.ca/posts/blaugust-intro), for posting your intro to remind me that I should, too! I do have an [about](/about) page that's sorely in need of an update, so I'll write a few fun facts about me for this post and hopefully update my about page at some point soon.

* **I've been to 44 U.S. states.** I only need to go to Hawaii, Nebraska, Wyoming, Arkansas, Mississippi, and Alabama to make my list complete! Sadly I've only been to 9 other countries outside of the U.S., but one day I'll travel to more of them.
* **I haven't lived in the same apartment/house/dwelling for ~3 years** since I moved out of my parents' house for college. Yes, I'm really, really good at packing and unpacking.
* **I played flute up until I graduated high school** and participated in a youth wind ensemble and other bands I had to audition for. I even had a solo once! I stopped playing in college because my college didn't have a concert band program, but I still own my flute and all my music.
* **I've done many fun things with my hair:** short pixie cuts, shoulder length fun, and medium-long (the longest my hair has been!), and I've dyed my hair platinum blonde, jet black, pink, blue, purple, red, and probably other colors I've forgotten by now. I love my longish blue/purple hair and I'm glad to still rock this style in my mid-40s.
* **I ran a mile in 6:44 in middle school** for my school's annual cross country race and got 3rd place for women in my entire grade. My picture was even in the local paper! I so wish I could run that fast now.
* **I taught myself how to knit left-handed**, so I knit the opposite way from almost everyone else (from right to left). When knitting cardigans, the left side becomes the right side and so on, so I always have to think about that when working on a more complicated project. It keeps me on my toes, at least!
* **I love to sing karaoke** and my favorite songs to sing are "Flagpole Sitta" by Harvey Danger, "My Own Worst Enemy" by Lit, and "Basketcase" by Green Day. Why yes, I was a teen in the 90s, how did you know?
