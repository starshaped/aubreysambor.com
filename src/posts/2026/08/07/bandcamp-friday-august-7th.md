Today is [Bandcamp Friday](https://isitbandcampfriday.com), one of the many days a year where [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com) waives revenue fees, ensuring artists get 100% of the cut of any album sales on the platform that day. I'm trying to buy more albums instead of using streaming services (I'm currently on Apple Music because I get it for free through my credit card), so I save up albums to buy on Bandcamp Fridays so the artists get all my money.

I participated in my first Bandcamp Friday earlier in the year. I've been buying just the digital copies of these albums because I don't have a CD, cassette, or record player to play physical media, but maybe one day that will change!

On to the albums I'm purchasing today:

*Ambiguous Desire* by [Arlo Parks](https://arloparks.bandcamp.com/album/ambiguous-desire)

I've listened to this album a couple times after it was released and it's different from the indie rock I usually gravitate toward. This album is soulful and catchy and has fantastic beats, so I anticipate listening to it all the time.

*bitknot* by [Feeble Little Horse](https://feeblelittlehorse.bandcamp.com/album/bitknot)

This is more along the lines of what I usually listen to, shoegazey lo-fi indie rock. This is a fun album to drive to and I've put it on while stuck in traffic on the Pike! Anything to make a traffic jam fun, and good music is the best way to pass the time.
