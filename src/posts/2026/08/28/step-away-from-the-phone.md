Remember how I said [yesterday](/where-i-used-to-walk/) I was going to write today's blog post in the morning?

Did I do that?

No.

So here I am yet again, 7pm on Friday night, rushing to blog so I can relax on my couch and listen to podcasts. Why can't I make myself get up and blog in the mornings?

I mean, I know the culprit and it is my phone, the device that sadly calls to me when I wake up in the morning, and even though I only check Mastodon and my RSS feeds when I wake up, it wastes enough time that I don't have time to blog before I work out and shower and eat and all the other important morning things I do.

I need to change this. I truly need to stop going on my phone before bed as that affects my sleep, and going on it in the mornings as that affects my morning routine.

I'm going to give this a try starting tonight. No phone in bed, Aubrey! Break the habit!
