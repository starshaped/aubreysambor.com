It's 5:00pm on August 1st and I read [Jedda's post](https://notes.jeddacp.com/blaugust-2026-year-3/) and [hexephre's post](https://hexephre.com/blogposts/260801) about Blaugust, a yearly event where participants blog every day for a month, and decided to join in on the fun and kickstart writing in this blog again.

## What will I write about?

I'm trying to figure this out both for this month and in general, because I've allowed myself to get in my head about not blogging about professional web development stuff because I feel like I *have* to, not because I *want* to. I might write from prompts, I might post about my knitting (if I remember to actually, you know, take pictures of what I'm making), and I might finally write more "[I'm too cool for this album](/tags/i-m-too-cool-for-this-album/)" posts. I'm hoping this will inspire me to tweak my blog layout and styles and maybe even move my blog repository from Github to Codeberg and from Netlify to a host yet to be determined. The sky's the limit!

## Will I keep up with this?

I'd like to say I will, but I honestly don't know. I haven't blogged regularly in a long time and I really haven't blogged much in the past two years because I've gone through a bunch of hard things right in a row, but now I want to try again. I didn't expect to be writing a blog post today but here I am, and I want to make it a goal to post every day, even if it means I write a bunch of posts in one day and post them later. I mainly want to get out of my head and *write*.

***

All right, here I go! Wish me luck this month!
