...and I feel weird about it sometimes.

I deleted my Facebook account (for, like, the third or fourth time) a couple years ago, deleted my Twitter account, have never even watched anything on Tiktok, and while I still have an Instagram account, I don't use it and don't have the app on my phone. I'm 100% happy with my choices and am super happy with Mastodon as essentially the only social media I use, but sometimes I feel out of place with my peers.

I have no idea what recipes are viral on Tiktok. I know nothing about the ads people see on Instagram. There've been a few times where I've missed news stories that everyone seemed to know about and I didn't, because apparently some of these stories were all over Instagram and Tiktok. (Related, I probably need to find a news source that isn't terrible!)

I feel a weird kind of FOMO sometimes when everyone I know posts on Instagram and I don't, but then I remember that posting on IG and reacting to people's posts and stories didn't make me feel closer to them. Spending real life time with my friends or having a virtual chat or reaching out via text or DM does. Maybe this is what the big tech companies want us to think so we use their services, who knows?

Either way, I'm happy with my social media consumption. I'm happy with the community I've built on Mastodon and I enjoy the interactions I have with everyone there. I'm so happy so many of my Mastodon friends are also participating in Blaugust, so I've been reading so many delightful posts and getting to know my Masto pals better. 

I don't need mainstream social media because I'm happy with what I have right now.
