Usually I'd be posting a longer week in review here, but I'm so tired. The longer the month has gone, the more burned out I've gotten from blogging every day, so I'm unsure I'll participate in Blaugust next year.  Most of my days this month have been me thinking "When am I going to blog? I need to write today's post and I really don't want to be at my computer right now", and it's pretty exhausting.

I'm glad I kept these week in review posts going through the month of August, though, but they're really not my thing. I've tried writing these week in review posts over and over through the years, but honestly, it takes me awhile to get through books and I knit bigger projects, so my updates are pretty boring. I still want to blog, but this is not it. Keeping a [/now page](https://nownownow.com) is more my speed, so I'll probably end up doing that instead. Once I take a little break after Blaugust is finished, of course.

So, yeah. These week in review posts were a good experiment, but all in all, not for me.
