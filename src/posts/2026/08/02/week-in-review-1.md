Since I'm doing [Blaugust](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/) this month, I'm going to attempt to use Sundays as my 'week in review' days. I think of Monday as the start of my week, so Sunday's a good day to wrap things up. Let's get to it.

## Making
I started the [Lillesol sweater](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/lillesol) by Isabell Kraemer (I love all her patterns!) using [Sea Change Fibers](https://www.seachangefibers.com) Fjord Tweed Fingering weight yarn in the Driftwood colorway, which is a night light gray. It is a super fun pattern with an interesting construction and I'm hoping to post more about this sweater early next week!

## Watching
* All the reality TV! I'm watching Big Brother 28 and listening to [live feed podcasts on RHAP](https://robhasawebsite.com/shows/big-brother-podcast-rhap/). I tried getting into Big Brother last year but I didn't like it, but something changed this year and I'm enjoying this silly show.
* I'm also watching season 20 of Married at First Sight and just finished rewatching Survivor: Millennials vs Gen X. What made me laugh about this Survivor season is that at least one of the people on the Gen X tribe was born in 1982, so definitely NOT in Gen X!
  
## Reading
* I'm almost done with [The Lion Women of Tehran](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/54d49b26-f846-4c43-a2dd-2031c3750f97) by Marjan Kamali and I think it's fine. I just finished [The Island of Sea Women](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/11cb3cc9-da00-47fc-b252-bb3644131680) by Lisa See and the general premise of both books, historical fiction where two girls who become friends and how things change for them and their friendship, was similar, so maybe I needed to take a break between reading these books.
* I'm also almost done with [Careless People](https://app.thestorygraph.com/books/757bf54f-a0fb-47d5-a2cb-12ff8f1fd43a) by Sarah Wynn-Williams and it's furthering the reasons why I don't have a Facebook account. Some of the shit that goes down in this book is unreal.

## Listening
I got back to my electronic roots roots this week and listened to [Group Therapy by Above & Beyond](https://music.apple.com/us/artist/above-beyond/20318188/compilation-albums) (Apple Music link), a weekly mix/radio show made by the electronic music group Above & Beyond. I've never really been into Above & Beyond but I love a lot of artists on their labels, Anjunabeats and Anjunadeep, so I love to check out the mixes every once in awhile. They're great to drive to as well, especially when stuck in traffic (which is usually the case when I drive to my knit nights after work!)
