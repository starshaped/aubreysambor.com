One thing that's kept me from blogging recently is how I come across on this blog. I've been working as a web developer professionally for over 20 years, so I should write about cool tech things on my site, right? Shouldn't I be talking about all my side projects, the apps I write in my spare time, the ideas I create and post on Github?

Nope.

I've never been someone who's been big on side projects (besides this blog, of course!). I love tinkering with my blog and with [11ty (now known as Build Awesome)](https://www.11ty.dev) and learning new technologies and testing new CSS techniques when I can. Besides that, though, I don't spend much time on my computer outside of work hours and have many non-computer hobbies that take up my time in the evenings and weekends. 

Sometimes I feel like that makes me an outlier in tech, that coding is not my primary hobby. I don't bring my computer on vacations or when I visit family. I have zero Github projects besides my blog (and I'm planning on moving to Codeberg sometime soonish). [I give talks at a couple conferences a year](/speaking), so [I do have a few pens on Codepen](https://codepen.io/starshaped) to show code examples.

After work I'd rather be knitting, or spinning, or journalling, or making my planner pretty and functional. I always need a break from the computer after I'm done for the day and after I'm done making dinner and then cleaning up after said dinner, I don't want to hop on my computer and write more code. I love focusing on sweater patterns or getting into the meditative nature of spinning on my spinning wheel, or getting thoughts out in my journal. 

My tagline on this blog has been "*A blog about knitting, front end development, and other assorted things*" and I need to update this to be more reflective of what I *actually* post. Because I'd rather be writing about trips I take, things I'm making, or conferences I attend more than coding. Maybe this will change someday and I'll post code examples on my site, but right now, I want to blog about my non-computer related hobbies. That's what really matters to me right now.
